About Sophia

Hello, I’m Sophia, the founder of Ad Astra Latin Tutoring.

I’m a passionate Classicist and teacher of Latin and Classics, with a rare Congratulatory First in Literae Humaniores (Classics) from the University of Oxford and experience teaching at both primary and secondary level. I’m currently completing my PGCE in Latin with Classics at King’s College London.

All my lessons are bespoke and tailored to each student's individual goals, strengths, interests and learning styles, aiming to help you develop your confidence, curiosity and a lasting appreciation for the richness of the Classical world.
